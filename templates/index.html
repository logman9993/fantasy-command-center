ESPN private league sync
1. Install the Command Center ESPN Sync extension from the V8.2 package.
2. Sign into ESPN normally and open your fantasy football team page.
3. Click the extension and press Sync current ESPN team.
4. Command Center will reopen here with your ESPN League HQ analysis.
Security: the extension uses ESPN's already-authenticated page session. Command Center receives league JSON, not your ESPN password or cookie values.
